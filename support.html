Free version and unlocking

Your first 3 scripts are free, with every feature; deleting one does not give the slot back. One purchase unlocks unlimited scripts; there is no subscription. On the Mac App Store, use Unlock under Set up › License in the app, or Restore Purchases on another Mac signed in with the same Apple ID. On Windows and direct Mac downloads, buy a license key, then paste it in under Set up › License. A key works on up to 2 of your own computers; use Deactivate on this computer before moving it.

Lost your key?

Search your email for "Lemon Squeezy" and "Just Prompter". If you cannot find it, email us from the address you used at checkout and we will resend it.

Quick answers

The prompter opened on the wrong screen. Choose the display at the top of the right-hand panel, then click Open prompter again.

Text is backwards on my monitor but right in the preview. That is the mirror setting for your glass. Turn Mirror horizontal off under “On the glass” in the right-hand panel if you are reading directly from the monitor.

Keys do nothing. Click outside the script editor first; keys are ignored while you are typing in it. Space plays and pauses.

The phone can't open the remote. The phone and the computer must be on the same Wi-Fi. If the Wi-Fi blocks devices from seeing each other (common in hotels), share your phone's hotspot to the computer instead.

How do I colour words? Select them in the script and click a colour above the page. The app writes a tag like {red|slow down} into the text; you can type one yourself. Colours: amber, red, green, blue, grey, and bold. Plain removes the tag.

The remote doesn't vibrate when I tap. On Android, Do Not Disturb silences vibration for web pages; turn it off or allow it. On iPhone, the tap feedback needs iOS 17.4 or later.

A PDF imported empty. The PDF has no text layer (it is a scan). Run it through OCR first, or paste the text.

I use two computers. How do I get the same scripts on both? Under Set up › Scripts, click Choose folder… and pick a folder in iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive. On the other computer, Mac or Windows, choose the same folder. Both then share one set of scripts, and a change on one shows up on the other within seconds while the app is open. Your current scripts are copied into the folder, and any already there are kept. If both computers change the same script at the same moment, the last save wins; if your sync service keeps both versions, the extra one appears as its own script. Each computer still needs its own unlock: a license key covers up to 2 of your computers, and on the Mac App Store use Restore Purchases.

The app says my scripts folder is not available. It could not open the folder you chose, usually because the sync app is not running or a drive is unplugged, so it is showing the scripts on this computer for now. Start the sync app or reconnect the drive, then quit and reopen Just Prompter.

The app showed an error. Choose Help › Show Error Log, and email that file to us with a line about what you were doing.
